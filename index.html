ASIAN GAMES 2026

India ends day 7 with 30 medals

The 7th day of the ongoing Asian Games saw a plethora of medals come India's way, one of the more successful days for the Indian contingent, as two gold medals were secured by the men and women's Kabaddi teams propelling India's medal tally to 30.

The Asian Games 2026 sees the Indian contingent put in their best performance in hopes of adding more medals to their tally. It is interesting to note that day 6 ended with the Indian contingent in 12th place in the standings with 26 medals to its name. The 7th day ended with India rising up to 10th in the medal tally as the nation now has 30 medals to its name. With four golds, 12 silvers, and 14 bronze medals, India continued to rise the ranks with some brilliant showings in a variety of sports.

The day began on a positive note for India as Sawan Barwal won the silver medal in the marathon, with Chinappa and Senthilkumar winning the bronze medal in the mixed squash doubles clash. Furthermore, the men's and women's Kabbadi teams won their respective finals against Iran and brought home to gold medals for India, defending their crown in spectacular fashion.
